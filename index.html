Club of Munchkins

Club of Munchkins is a club with the sole purpose to talk about the Munchkin Cat breed.
In this club we often share articles and content related to Munchkin Cats.

3 Facts About Munchkin Cats

  • Munchkin Cats can be oftened referred to as the "Sausage Cat", due to their long appearance. (Source).
  • Munchkin Cats are very intelligent and inquisitive, meaning you can train them to play fetch or walk on a leash. (Source).
  • Munchkin Cats can develop Health problems over time or sometimes at birth, conditions such as: Lordosis or Osteoarthritis. (Source).
