# What does the report tell you about the Data Set?
It tells you the total amount of trails, the total amount of open trails, the total amount of closed trails, the total amount of kilometres for all the open trails, the name of the longest open trail, and the amount of moderate, easy, and hard ratings found in all the trails of the data set.
