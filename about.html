About Club of Munchkins

In the Club of Munchkins, we engage in any and all content related to the Munchkin Cat breed. Our interest in this breed is largely due to their intelligence and social behavior, often being compared to the personality you'd find in a dog. On this page, it will go into more depth about these interests.

A Munchkin's Traits (Intelligence)

Owners of Munchkin cats often boast about their feline's intelligence, it is a big talking point when it comes to the breed. It is often said that Munchkin cats ressemble that of a dog due to their loyalty and intution. Typically, Munchkin cats have a desire for mental stimulation, which allows for them to be trainable in a variety of ways. In addition to that, they also have impressive problem solving capabilities, which makes interacting with them much more engaging. (Source)

A Munchkin's Traits (Social Behavior)

Munchkin cats are just as social as they are intelligent. Their social ability and connection with their owners is largely related to their intelligence. As in order to maintain mental stimulation, they interact with their owners. Munchkin cats also maintain excellent social connection by their impression memory, studies have shown that they possess the ability to remember experiences from months to even years ago. Their memory also allows for them to form strong bonds with their owners, displaying signs of loyalty and affection beyond the typical expectation of a pet. (Source)
